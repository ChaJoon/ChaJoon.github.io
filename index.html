85%

The RFID chip's datasheet only documented standard capacitive sensors, so I worked out the interface from first principles, hand-fabricated the flexible PCB, and validated the complete system wirelessly detecting breathing at 100 cm range.

NEM Resonator Characterisation

71%
PythonCOMSOLLock-in amplifierMATLAB

Characterised a doubly-clamped silicon NEM resonator by lock-in amplifier measurement, extracting resonant frequency and quality factor and identifying Duffing nonlinearity from AC power sweeps. Built a Python pipeline to fit the measured data, then validated it against a COMSOL beam simulation agreeing with the measured frequency to within 3%.

3D-Printed Sensor Package for a Piezoelectric Accelerometer

71%
SolidWorks3D printingNI MultisimLCR meter

Designed a parametric sensor housing in SolidWorks and a two-stage charge amplifier circuit, then validated the assembly on an electromagnetic shaker rig across three mass conditions. The measured resonance came out roughly five times lower than the datasheet value; I traced it to the shaker's own mechanical resonance dominating the measurement, rather than treating it as a failed result.

Signal Conditioning for an Engine Vibration Accelerometer

70%
NI MultisimAnalogue designManual calculation

Designed a signal-conditioning chain for a piezoresistive accelerometer. My first differential-amplifier design didn't match calculations by a wide margin — rather than retuning components, I traced the fault to a bridge-impedance mismatch and replaced it with an instrumentation-amplifier topology that cut the signal loss from 78% to under 1%.
